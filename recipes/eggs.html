Eggs Recipe

Description

Eggs are one of the most delecious and quick foods out there. you can make it in couple of minutes and it can give you high amount of protien and energy.

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Gas
  • Oil
  • pepper
  • Tomatos
  • Onion

Steps

  1. boil oil in a pan
  2. put onion and tomatos
  3. steer them to make a syrup
  4. drop 2 - 4 (as much as the syrup can handle) eggs
  5. stir them the way you want
  6. add pepper
  7. add salt
